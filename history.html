THE SAAF STORY

A manufacturing journey built on quality, capability and trust.

Established in 2015, SAAF Leather Products began with a clear vision to build a dependable leather manufacturing business from Ambur, Tamil Nadu.

Since then, we have continued to develop our people, manufacturing capabilities and approach to product development while working with clients in international markets.

OUR JOURNEY

From foundation to global manufacturing.

Key stages in the development of SAAF Leather Products.

2015

THE BEGINNING

SAAF Leather Products is founded

SAAF Leather Products was founded in 2015 by Aafaque Mohammed K and Sadeed Ahmed M.

The company was established in Ambur, Tamil Nadu, with a vision to build a trusted leather manufacturing business focused on quality craftsmanship, dependable production and client satisfaction.

2018

EXPANDING THE TEAM

A new chapter of development

In 2018, Mohammed Taukhir M joined SAAF Leather Products.

His addition strengthened the team as the company continued developing its manufacturing and client relationships and pursuing opportunities in international markets.

GROWTH

DEVELOPING CAPABILITIES

Expanding product development

Over time, SAAF developed its capabilities across leather footwear, leather goods and leather garments, while continuing to work with skilled people and experienced artisans.

Our approach evolved beyond production alone, incorporating product development, sampling, material selection and technical preparation for client requirements.

OEM

MANUFACTURING PARTNERSHIPS

Supporting brands from concept to production

SAAF's manufacturing approach developed around supporting brands, designers, retailers and private-label businesses with product development and manufacturing.

Our capabilities now include footwear, leather bags, leather garments and distinctive handwoven leather constructions developed according to client designs and specifications.

TODAY

WHERE WE ARE TODAY

Serving international clientss

Today, SAAF Leather Products manufactures ladies' high-fashion leather footwear, leather goods and leather garments.

Our products are manufactured for clientss across Australia, Europe and the United States.

With a workforce of 95+ women and men, we continue to combine skilled craftsmanship with organized manufacturing to support international client requirements.

LOOKING AHEAD

Continuing the journey.

SAAF continues to develop its people, product capabilities and manufacturing processes as we work toward becoming a stronger long-term partner for global brands and private-label businesses.
