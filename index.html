SAAF LEATHER PRODUCTS

A manufacturing partner built around your requirements.

Established in 2015, SAAF Leather Products is a manufacturer and exporter of ladies' high-fashion leather footwear, leather goods and leather garments, based in Ambur, India.

We partner with brands, designers, retailers and private-label businesses to develop products from initial concepts and specifications through sampling and production.
